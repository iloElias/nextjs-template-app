# Math Formulas Test

## Inline Math

The quadratic formula is $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$ and Euler's identity is $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

Einstein's famous equation: $E = mc^2$

## Display Math (Block)

The area of a circle:

$$A = \pi r^2$$

The Pythagorean theorem:

$$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$

Summation notation:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i = x_1 + x_2 + \ldots + x_n$$

Integral calculus:

$$\int_a^b f(x) \, dx$$

Matrix representation:

$$\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$$

## Mixed Content

You can combine text with formulas. For example, the derivative of $f(x) = x^2$ is $f'(x) = 2x$.

The fundamental theorem of calculus states:

$$\int_a^b f'(x) \, dx = f(b) - f(a)$$

This is widely used in mathematics and physics.

## Greek Letters and Symbols

Alpha: $\alpha$, Beta: $\beta$, Gamma: $\gamma$

Delta: $\Delta x$, Theta: $\theta$, Lambda: $\lambda$

## Fractions and Powers

Simple fraction: $\frac{1}{2}$

Nested fraction: $\frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{2}}$

Powers: $x^2$, $x^{10}$, $2^{2^{2^2}}$

## Subscripts and Superscripts

Variables: $x_1, x_2, x_n$

Combined: $x_i^2$

Chemical formula: $H_2O$, $CO_2$
